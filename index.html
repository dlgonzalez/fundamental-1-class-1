6 Rules for Success

Follow these rules to learn English fast and enjoy the class!

1. Cameras ON

We are a team. Turn on your camera so we can connect, see your smile, and communicate better!

2. Make Mistakes!

Do not be afraid. Mistakes are proof that you are trying. This is a safe space to practice.

3. Participate Actively

Speak up! Answer questions. Talk to your classmates. You learn English by opening your mouth.

4. Use Platforms

Check Q10 for grades. Practice in MacMillan. The platforms help you grow outside of class.

5. Ask Questions

If you don't understand, say: "Teacher, can you repeat?" or "I don't understand." I am here to help!

6. English Everywhere

Listen to music, watch movies, read internet posts in English. Bring English into your daily life!

Saying Hello & Goodbye

Now you know the rules! Let's start with the most important words in English: how to say hello and goodbye to people.

Vocabulary List

Greetings (Hello)

Hello / Hi / Hey (Any time)
Good morning (Before 12:00 PM)
Good afternoon (12:00 PM to 5:00 PM)
Good evening (After 5:00 PM)
How are you? (Formal Question)
How's it going? (Informal Question)
What's up? (Very Informal)

Farewells (Goodbye)

Goodbye / Bye (Standard)
See you later (Informal)
See you tomorrow (Next day)
Take care (Friendly)
Catch you later (Very Informal)
Have a good day! (Polite)
Good night (Going to sleep)
Note: "Good evening" is a greeting (Hello). "Good night" is a farewell (Goodbye/Going to sleep).
